CRA source deduction remittances

Grouped by the month pay was actually sent. Covers CPP, EI, and federal/provincial tax withheld — plus the employer's matching CPP and EI share. This tool calculates the amount only; you still remit it yourselves through CRA My Business Account or your bank. Assumes monthly-remitter status (due the 15th of the following month) — confirm your actual remitting frequency with CRA if unsure.

Year-end T4 slips

Built from the same YTD totals as the paystubs — only sent (not draft) pay periods count. Boxes 14/16/16A/18/22/24/26 are populated; box 10 is fixed to ON since this tool only supports Ontario. Full SINs aren't stored (only the last 3 digits), so double-check the SIN before filing.
